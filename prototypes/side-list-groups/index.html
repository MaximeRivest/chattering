Groups in the side list

A working prototype with your real open list (8 Oct, evening). Click, drag, and type in the column on the left: it behaves as the design says. Nothing here touches the live app.

Try this

  • Hover a row: ⊕ moves it into a group. Or press g with the keyboard cursor on it.
  • In the picker, the group you used last is first: g Enter puts a row where the previous one went.
  • Drag one row onto another: the two become a new group.
  • Ctrl/Shift-click to pick several, then g.
  • Click a group's name to fold it. Double-click to rename.
  • The arrange button by ＋ new: Tidy up proposes groups for you, or switch to By project.

The rules, in short

  • A group is a name you give to some open rows. Rows outside groups stay on top, as today.
  • A folded group is your back burner: out of the way, never out of reach.
  • A folded group still shows a new reply or a stopped run, under its header. Once read, the row tucks back in (when you move on, not while you read it).
  • The header says the rest: how many, who is working, open questions.
  • Closing a row keeps its group: if the assistant replies later it comes back where you put it.
  • Same list, same groups, same folds on laptop, phone and tablet.
  • A fork or a new conversation started from a grouped one joins that group; ＋ new at the top stays loose.

Keyboard (list focused)

  • ↑↓ rows and headers · Enter open / fold
  • ← fold the group you're in · → unfold
  • g move to group · Space pick several · Esc clear
  • Shift+← fold every group · Shift+G arrange
  • F2 rename a group · Delete close (with Undo)

Other shapes I considered, and why not

Decisions, and what each one costs

Names you choose, not a fixed “back burner”

Folding is the back burner, so no special bucket is built in. “Later” is only the name the picker offers first. If the habit fades, nothing in the app was designed around it.

Cost: one decision (a name) the first time; the picker’s suggestion makes it one key.

A folded group lets replies and stops through

Otherwise you’d stop trusting the fold and keep everything open, which is today’s problem. A question (?) does not peek once read: back-burner work often ends with a question, and it would never fold.

Cost: a reply in a group you meant to forget still shows. If that ever bothers you, a per-group “quiet” switch is the next step, not a v1 feature.

Loose rows on top, groups below

New conversations land loose, where your attention is. A group never pushes a working row off screen. New groups go first among the groups; drag a tray’s header to reorder.

Cost: a group you’re actively in sits below the loose rows. Pin still lifts any row to the very top.

By project is a view, not a second set of groups

It lives behind the arrange button, says it’s on (a dot on the button and a line at the top of the list), and leaves your groups untouched. Rows lose their now-redundant project name.

Cost: two arrangements means two shapes for the same list; that’s why it’s one click away and never the default.

Tidy up proposes; you apply

The easy “sort this out for me” action. Plain rules, shown before anything moves: rows in use stay loose, two or more from one project become a folded group, single leftovers go to “Later”. Undo afterwards too.

Cost: no model call, so it can’t spot cross-project efforts (“Lily’s setup”). That’s what naming suggestions are for.

Suggested names

Rows from one project: the project’s name. Rows from several: a small model reads the titles and proposes one (“Lily’s setup”). It appears a moment later and never replaces what you typed.

Cost: one short model call when you open the picker on mixed rows; off when AI work is off, like titles.

What it would take to build

State

  • Two maps in the shared list state (agent-read.json, beside pinned): groups[id] = {name, order, folded} and member[key] = id. Same deltas, same sync, same Undo as closing.
  • Membership survives a close, so a reply brings the row back into its group. An empty group stays hidden for 30 days, then goes; its name stays offered.
  • Keys are prefixed so files can join groups later (file:/path).

Where

  • renderAgentsPop(): sections instead of one flat list; reconcileList() already animates moves between them.
  • Row ⋯ menu gains “Move to group…”; the picker reuses the model picker’s type-to-filter pattern.
  • Phone sheet and e-ink: same list; bottom-sheet picker; no drag.
  • Tests: grouping/peek/tuck rules in agentread.test.js; the journey in open-list.test.js.
